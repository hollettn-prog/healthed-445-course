HEALTHED 445 | MODULE 2

Discussion: What Would Count as Learning?

Share a draft and examine the evidence

For this discussion, you're going to work backward from what students should learn to what they would need to show you.

Share your draft

  1. Choose a Consumer Health context and a grades 9 to 12 NHES performance indicator.
  2. Describe a plausible learner need. Label it as an assumption for planning. You don't need to collect data from a school.
  3. Write your learning goal and briefly describe the task you'd use to check it.
  4. Explain what an individual student would say, write, or demonstrate to show the intended learning.
  5. Tell us where you're still unsure. What part of the goal, task, or evidence needs another look?
