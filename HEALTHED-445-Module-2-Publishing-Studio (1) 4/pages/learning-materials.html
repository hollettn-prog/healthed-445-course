The Planning Example

This fictional case follows Ms. Rivera’s 26 eighth graders. After viewing two wellness posts, 18 students trust the post with more followers, six check who created it, and nine notice its date. A student can appear in more than one count.

Students have different levels of reading confidence, and some have limited internet access at home. Ms. Rivera has one 45-minute class. Printed posts and background details about their creators, evidence, and purposes are available.

Use the Standards

For Ms. Rivera’s eighth-grade example, consult NHES 3.8.4. The task addresses the validity and reliability portion of that indicator; it does not assess every part.

For your own Consumer Health activity, choose a grades 9–12 performance indicator. Explain which part your activity addresses. NHES 3.12.3 is relevant if you choose source evaluation.
