HEALTHED 445 • MODULE 0

Pre-Student Teaching

25 hours in health education

Pre-student teaching is your opportunity to move from observing health education instruction to leading it yourself. You'll work with a licensed health education teacher, practice teaching in their classroom, and receive feedback on your instruction.

You are expected to teach multiple health education lessons before your formal university supervisor observation. This is not an observation-only placement.

Your 25-Hour Experience

~5 hours

Observe a licensed health education teacher.

~20 hours

Practice and teach under the licensed teacher's supervision.

The hour breakdown is approximate. Your placement must total 25 hours specifically in health education.

How Pre-Student Teaching Works

1. Observe

Learn how your cooperating teacher manages the classroom, teaches health skills, and supports student learning.

2. Practice and Teach

Take an increasingly active role in instruction. Independently lead multiple health education lessons with your licensed cooperating teacher supervising.

3. Complete Your University Observation

A university-assigned supervisor observes you teaching health education, either in person or through an approved online arrangement.

4. Reflect and Finish

Review your feedback and complete the required pre-student teaching documentation.

An Important Conversation

At the beginning of your placement, tell your cooperating teacher that you need opportunities to independently teach multiple health education lessons before your university observation. Work together to plan when that will happen.
