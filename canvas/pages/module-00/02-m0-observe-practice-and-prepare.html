HEALTHED 445 • MODULE 0

Observe, Practice, and Prepare

Pre-Student Teaching Guide

Your pre-student teaching experience should gradually move you from observing your cooperating teacher to independently leading health education instruction.

You are not expected to teach perfectly. You are expected to practice, make instructional decisions, respond to students, and learn from feedback.

1. Observe Your Cooperating Teacher

During approximately your first five hours, observe how your licensed cooperating teacher:

  • Introduces health topics and connects them to students' lives.
  • Models health skills and provides opportunities for practice.
  • Creates a respectful environment for discussing sensitive topics.
  • Manages classroom routines, participation, and behavior.
  • Checks for understanding and provides feedback.

Ask questions about the instructional decisions you observe. What does your cooperating teacher do, and why?

2. Begin Practicing Teaching

After your initial observations, work with your cooperating teacher to take on more instructional responsibility.

Examples of supervised teaching practice

  • Lead an opening activity or lesson introduction.
  • Facilitate a discussion about a health-related decision.
  • Model a health skill, such as evaluating health information.
  • Guide students through skill practice and provide feedback.
  • Check students' understanding and adjust instruction.
  • Plan and lead a complete health education lesson.

3. Independently Teach Multiple Lessons

As you gain experience, take responsibility for planning and delivering complete health education lessons. Your licensed cooperating teacher remains present to supervise and support you.

Before teaching, discuss the learning goals, instructional activities, and how you will check student learning. Afterward, ask for feedback and identify what you would change.

Before your university observation: You should already have independently taught multiple health education lessons. Your university observation should not be your first experience leading a complete lesson.

4. Prepare for Your University Observation

Work with your cooperating teacher and university-assigned supervisor to identify an appropriate lesson and arrange the observation.

Choose a lesson that gives you an opportunity to demonstrate your teaching, including how you engage students, facilitate learning, manage the classroom, and assess understanding.

Review the observation instructions on the next page before finalizing arrangements.

Questions to Discuss With Your Cooperating Teacher

  • When can I begin leading parts of instruction?
  • Which lessons could I plan and teach independently?
  • What feedback would help me improve?
  • When would be an appropriate time for my university supervisor to observe?
