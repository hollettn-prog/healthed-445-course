HEALTHED 445 • MODULE 0

Reflect and Finish

Complete 25 hours specifically in health education: approximately five hours observing a licensed health education teacher and approximately 20 hours practicing and independently teaching multiple lessons under that teacher’s supervision.

Document and reflect

Keep your time log current and obtain the required sign-off. Use the existing Completed Time Log assignment and the field experience reflection activities already listed in Canvas. Use the current assignment settings for submission methods and due dates.

Submit your university supervisor’s completed observation form and reflection through the existing POST-Observation Feedback and Reflection assignment. Identify strengths, feedback, and the teaching practices you will work on next.

Before you finish

  • Confirm your 25 health education hours are documented.
  • Confirm you taught multiple lessons before the formal university observation.
  • Confirm the university observation and required documentation are complete.
  • Keep copies in your existing Teaching Toolkit.
