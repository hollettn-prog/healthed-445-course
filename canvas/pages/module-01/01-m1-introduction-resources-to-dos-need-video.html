Thinking Like a Health Educator

Introduction, Resources & To-Dos

Essential Question

What makes health education more than teaching students health content?

Why This Matters

Students can know what makes a health source trustworthy and still choose a social media post because it has more followers. As their teacher, how would you help them put what they know to use?

In this module, you'll compare content-focused and skills-based instruction, revisit your own experiences with health education, and begin building a teaching resource you can use later.
