Start with the Criteria

Judge the evidence for each criterion separately. A confident answer can contain errors; a short answer can still show useful reasoning. Give feedback that identifies a demonstrated strength and a specific next action. Keep the original response and supported revision distinct.

Riley’s Resource Task

In this fictional eighth-grade Community Health task, Riley wants physical activity, needs step-free access, and can spend no more than $5 a week. Riley stays at school until 4 p.m., has no arranged ride to another location, and then uses an accessible route home. Students compare the two cards, recommend a feasible option, identify an unknown, and say where to verify it.

Three Criteria

  • Fit: A feasible recommendation supported by Riley’s circumstances.
  • Comparison: Accurate details about both resources and an explanation of a meaningful difference.
  • Next step: A specific unknown and an appropriate place to verify it.

Describe each criterion as demonstrated, developing, or not yet demonstrated. Use not yet assessable when the response cannot be accessed or interpreted; this is an evidence status, not a claim about the learner’s skill.

Worked Feedback

For C: “You connected the club’s timing to Riley’s schedule. Add one accurate detail about Town Recreation and explain why that difference matters.” For B, address the incorrect cost and unsupported transportation claim. For D, obtain an accessible response before judging the skill.

Casey: A New Check

Casey wants free physical activity, needs step-free access, must take the dismissal bus, and has no arranged ride elsewhere. School Lunchtime Move is free, on site, and step-free, with a 12:10–12:30 lunchtime session; verify spaces with the school office. Community Evening Class is free and step-free but takes place two miles away in the evening and supplies no transportation; verify registration with the community program office.

Ask for the same comparison, justified recommendation, and verification step. Retain the same criteria and needed access supports. Do not supply the worked answer before collecting the individual response.
