HEALTHED 445 • MODULE 0

University Supervisor Observation

The formal observation by your university-assigned supervisor is the central clinical assessment of this field experience. Arrange an in-person observation or an approved online arrangement after you have independently taught multiple health education lessons under your licensed cooperating teacher’s supervision.

Before the observation

Work with your cooperating teacher and assigned university supervisor to select a lesson and arrange the observation. Use the existing pre-observation lesson plan assignment for submission instructions and timing.

During the observation

Teach the health education lesson so your university supervisor can observe how you engage students, facilitate learning, manage the classroom, and assess understanding. A submitted lesson plan supports the observation; it does not replace the observation.

After the observation

Review your university supervisor’s feedback. Follow the existing post-observation feedback and reflection assignment to submit the completed official observation form and your reflection.
