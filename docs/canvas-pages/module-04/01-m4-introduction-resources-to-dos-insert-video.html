Managing a Health Classroom

Introduction, Resources & To-Dos

Essential Question

What can I put in place so students can participate respectfully, and how will I respond when a concern comes up?

Module 4 Introduction

A routine can make it easier to join a discussion. A few words from the teacher can stop a harmful comment or make room for a student who needs another way to participate.

What You’ll Learn

  • Teach routines for discussion, questions, and participation.
  • Respond to a harmful comment while protecting students’ dignity and keeping the lesson moving.
  • Offer participation choices and recognize when a concern needs support beyond the lesson.
