Building Your Elementary PE Teacher Toolkit

Throughout this course, you will develop an Elementary Physical Education Teacher Toolkit by creating practical resources that align with each module's learning outcomes. Each assignment adds one artifact to your toolkit, allowing you to apply course concepts while building materials you can use during field experiences, student teaching, and your future career as an elementary physical educator.

As you progress through the course, you will also encounter examples of teaching challenges through the Maple Grove Elementary case study. While those scenarios provide opportunities to apply course concepts, the resources you create for your toolkit should reflect your own teaching philosophy, planning, and professional practice.

Getting Your Toolkit Ready

Download the zipped toolkit folder provided with this assignment, save a copy to your own Google Drive, and continue adding artifacts throughout the semester. At the end of the course, your completed toolkit will serve as a professional resource that supports your transition into teaching.

Follow these steps to download your toolkit folder from Canvas, upload it to Google Drive, and share it with your instructor.

Step 1: Download the Teaching Toolkit Folder.

  1. Open the file or folder in Canvas.
  2. Select the download icon, or right-click the item and choose Download. This saves a .zip file to your Downloads folder.
  3. Unzip the folder by double-clicking it. For Windows, right-click the file and select Extract All.

Step 2: Upload to Google Drive

  1. Go to UWW's Google Drive login and sign in with your NetID.
  2. Select + New, then choose Folder upload.
  3. Select the folder from your Downloads and upload it.
  4. Update the name of the folder to include your full name (ex. TedLasso PEPROF437 Teaching Toolkit). To do this, right click on the folder in Drive >> Rename.

Step 3: Share with Your Instructor

  1. Right-click the uploaded file or folder in Drive and select Share.
  2. Enter your instructor's email address.
  3. Set permission to Viewer or Commenter, as instructed for this assignment.
  4. Select Send.

💡 Tip: Double-check that your instructor's email is entered correctly before sending, and confirm the folder appears in your Google Drive under "Shared."
