HEALTHED 445 • MODULE 1

When Knowing Isn't Enough

Discussion

Think back to a health lesson. What did students have a chance to practice?

Start the Conversation

Choose a health learning experience you remember as a student or have observed. Briefly describe the topic and what students actually did.

Would you describe the instruction as content-focused, skills-based, or a combination? Explain your interpretation using the student actions. What could the teacher tell about learning from the work students produced, and what would still be unclear?

Suggest one manageable teaching change that would help students practice or demonstrate a health skill.

Respond to One Classmate

Choose a post that gives you something to think about. Offer another interpretation, ask a question about the evidence of learning, or build on the proposed change. Refer to something specific in their example.

Choose an Example You're Comfortable Sharing

Focus on instruction and student work. Leave out names and personal health details. If you don't recall a suitable experience, use Approach A from the knowledge check as your example.

Willie's Teaching Tip

What evidence supports your interpretation of the lesson? What might a different observer notice?
