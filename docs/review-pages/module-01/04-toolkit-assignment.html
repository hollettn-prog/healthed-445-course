Five-Step Health Skill Teaching Guide

Create a short guide to the five steps for teaching health skills. Save it in your existing Toolkit so you can use it when planning future lessons.

1. Open Your Toolkit

Open your existing Pre-Service Teaching Toolkit and locate the HEALTHED 445 section. Name this resource M1: Five-Step Health Skill Teaching Guide.

[INSTRUCTOR: Insert the verified Toolkit interactive link here. This page does not save entries to the Toolkit by itself.]

2. Create Your Guide

Use the five-step sequence from M1 Learning Materials. Keep the same step names and order. For each step, include:

  • Purpose: How this step helps students learn a health skill.
  • Teacher cue: Something you could say or do.
  • Student action: What students would do during the step.

Use M1's fictional ninth-grade social media example throughout. Students compare sources and justify a judgment using creator, evidence, and purpose.

Keep your guide short enough to use when planning. Include the source of the five-step sequence so you can return to it.

3. Add a Learning Check

Finish with a brief answer: What could each student say, write, or demonstrate to show use of the skill? Use a new health post so you can see their reasoning, not just their recall of your example.

4. Save and Submit

Save the guide in your existing Toolkit and reopen it to check that the full response is there. Submit the same guide in Canvas as one Word document or PDF. Saving in the Toolkit and submitting in Canvas are separate actions.

If your Toolkit uses a shared Drive file, check that your instructor has permission to open it before submitting any link. Do not change permissions to public access.

Before submitting, check the five steps, student actions, and individual learning check.

Willie's Teaching Tip

Could you hand this guide to another teacher and have them understand what students would practice at each step?
