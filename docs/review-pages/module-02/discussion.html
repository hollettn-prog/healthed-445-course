A good learning task should give you evidence that students can do what your learning goal describes. In this discussion, you'll share an early draft, help a classmate think through their plan, and use the feedback to strengthen your own.

How the Pieces Connect

1. Learner Need

2. NHES Indicator

3. Learning Goal

What should students learn?

4. Task & Evidence

How will they show it?
