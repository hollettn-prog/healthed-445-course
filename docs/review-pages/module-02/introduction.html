What Would Count as Learning?

In this module, you'll look at how teachers can tell whether students have actually learned a health skill and not just whether they remember content.

You'll connect a learning goal to a task that gives students a chance to show what they can do.

Willie's Teaching Tip

Before choosing an assessment, ask yourself: What would a student actually say, write, or demonstrate to show they've learned the skill?

Before you finish

Make sure your discussion response identifies the learning goal, describes the assessment task, and explains what individual student evidence would demonstrate learning.
